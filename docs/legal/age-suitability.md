# Age Suitability

The Hub is rated **13+**. We set that rating ourselves, above the rating
Apple's content questionnaire calculated, because our own policy requires it.
This page explains why.

## Why 13+

- **Athletes under 13 cannot create an account.** The sign-up flow asks for a
  date of birth and blocks anyone under 13, directing them to have a parent or
  guardian create and manage the profile instead.
- **The app connects student-athletes with adult college coaches.** Approved
  coaches can message athletes, so this is not an environment intended for
  young children, regardless of content.
- **It collects real personal information** — name, date of birth, school,
  academic scores, photos, and contact details — which is not appropriate to
  solicit from young children.

## What the app does not contain

There is no violence, sexual content, profanity, horror, gambling, or
references to alcohol, tobacco, or drugs. There are no in-app purchases, no
advertising, and no third-party tracking.

## Protections for minors

- **Guardian consent is required to publish.** No profile for an athlete under
  18 can be made visible to coaches without a parent or guardian's name,
  email, and a recorded consent date. This is enforced on our servers, not
  just in the app.
- **Coaches are verified before they see anyone.** Coach accounts cannot
  self-activate. Every application is reviewed and approved by our staff.
- **Profiles are private by default.** An athlete chooses when to publish, and
  can unpublish at any time. Contact details are shown only to approved
  coaches who have saved the athlete's profile.
- **Report and block are built into every conversation**, and reports are
  reviewed within 24 hours.
- **Accounts can be deleted in the app**, which permanently removes the
  profile, photos, messages, and consent records.
