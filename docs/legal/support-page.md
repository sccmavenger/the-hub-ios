# Support

## Contact us

Questions, problems, or privacy requests — email info@summithoops.net.
We reply within two business days.

Reporting something urgent about a minor's safety? Put "URGENT" in the
subject line and we will prioritize it.

## Common questions

**How do I delete my account?**
In the app, go to More → Account → Delete Account. This permanently removes
the profile, photos, messages, and login.

**Who can see my profile?**
Only approved college coaches, and only after you publish it. Contact details
are shown only to coaches who have saved your profile.

**My athlete is under 18.**
Publishing requires a parent or guardian's recorded consent, including their
name and email.

**How do I report or block someone?**
Open the conversation, tap the menu in the top right, and choose Report or
Block. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours.

**Are coaches verified?**
Yes. Every coach account is manually reviewed and approved by our staff
before it can see any athlete.
